# Auto-Documentation Enforcer

**Activation:** Always On

Whenever you complete a major structural change, implement a core piece of logic from a workflow, or introduce a new dependency, you must automatically delegate the documentation task to the `tech-lead` subagent. 

Do not consider a workflow step "complete" until the `tech-lead` has updated the `knowledge_base/` files. Ensure your code is thoroughly commented so the `tech-lead` can accurately interpret your architectural decisions.
